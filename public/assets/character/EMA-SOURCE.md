# EMA female character

Author: THEUNSEENVULGA
Source: https://theunseenvulga.itch.io/3d-character-riggedema
Downloaded: 2026-10-12
License: CC0, as stated on the author's listing (free personal/commercial use and modification).

The female-explorer.glb asset is EMA's original mesh, Mixamo skeleton, and embedded
textures, with the project's Quaternius motion clips retargeted onto her skeleton.
Clothing material tints restore the author's preview palette because the supplied
GLB uses a grayscale cavity atlas for the clothing base color.

Motion source: the project's Quaternius adventurer, CC0.
Original EMA download: scripts/art/ema/EMA-original.glb.
Rebuild: node scripts/art/ema/retarget.mjs
The character remains distinct from the male model, including her face, hands,
body geometry, hair, clothing, skin weights, and skeleton.
