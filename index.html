How to explore

  • WS — walk forward / backward
  • AD or ←→ — rotate camera · ↑↓ — tilt camera
  • C — resume camera follow from behind
  • SHIFT — run
  • SPACE — jump
  • Drag mouse / click to lock — look around · scroll to zoom
  • E — interact near a beacon or unlock the prison gate
  • 1–5 or top nav — fast travel
  • H — toggle this help · Esc — close panels
